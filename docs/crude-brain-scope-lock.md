# Crude Brain scope lock

Current focus is NO-NEWS Crude research only.

Do not integrate `crude_news_intelligence`, historical headlines, EIA surprises, Hormuz state, war/ceasefire state, sanctions, OPEC events, supply disruptions, demand events or any other news-derived field into Crude Brain decisions until the no-news Crude Current Mind has been built, backtested and frozen.

The already merged Crude news-intelligence modules remain research infrastructure only and are intentionally disconnected from this baseline path.

The eventual comparison must preserve the frozen no-news decisions' dates/click schedule and alter only point-in-time news availability. This is required to measure incremental value from news rather than co-tuning the baseline with news outcomes.
